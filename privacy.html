Festora Privacy Policy

Festora is a personal iOS app for holiday decoration seasons, room-by-room checklists, placement counts, closeout recovery tracking, reference photos, and reuse templates. This Privacy Policy explains how Festora handles information on your device.

Scope

This policy applies to the Festora mobile application distributed on the App Store. Festora is designed as an on-device organizer. You do not need a Festora cloud account to create seasons, attach photos, dictate notes, or export local summaries.

Information stored on your device

When you use Festora, the following categories of content may be stored locally in the app sandbox:

  • Season titles, area labels, task titles, planned and placed quantities, recovered and damaged counts, skip reasons, and free-form notes you enter.
  • Photo copies you capture with the camera or select from your photo library as decoration or closeout references.
  • Optional dictated note text created when you use microphone access with speech recognition.
  • Local preference flags such as onboarding completion, first-home permission prompt state, and gift credit balances.
  • Verified in-app purchase transaction identifiers needed for local entitlement bookkeeping on this device.

Festora does not upload your season photos, voice-derived notes, or checklist values to a Festora business server as part of the core checklist workflow.

Permissions

Festora requests system permissions in context:

  • Notifications — a system prompt may appear when you first reach the home Records tab. Festora does not assign a notification center delegate for product push workflows and does not schedule or deliver notifications after the prompt.
  • Camera — when you tap Take Photo on a task to capture a decoration or room reference.
  • Photo Library — when you choose reference photos or save an image to Photos.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition — when you start dictation for a task note.

Festora does not request App Tracking Transparency and does not use advertising tracking identifiers for product workflows. If you deny a permission, features that depend on it stay unavailable while the rest of the app continues to work.

Purchases

Optional consumable gift packs are processed by Apple In-App Purchase. Apple handles payment credentials. Festora stores purchase results locally so pack credits can be applied on this device. Festora does not offer a restore purchases control for these consumable packs.

Sharing and exports

If you use export or the system share sheet, you choose what leaves the app. Copies you save or send outside Festora are outside Festora control once delivered to another app or location.

Analytics and advertising

Festora does not sell personal information. This package does not run advertising attribution or IDFA-based tracking workflows.

Data retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete a season or attached media, use Log Out / Clear local data in Settings, or remove the app. Clearing local data removes seasons, media stored by Festora, purchase history stored by the app, and preference flags from this installation.

Children

Festora is intended for a general audience that can manage personal photos and home decoration records. Do not store sensitive information about children in photos or notes.

Contact

For privacy questions about Festora, use the support channel listed on the App Store product page for Festora.
